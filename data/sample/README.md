# `data/sample/`

Reserved for a small demonstration bundle: the stored detections of one or two videos and
their video list, so that the tracking and counting steps can be watched running without the
full video archive or the model weights.

**The bundle has not been added.** It needs the authors' and the Ontario Ministry of Natural
Resources' agreement to publish stored detections for specific videos (see item A8 in
`docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_REVIEW.md`). Until then this folder is empty and nothing in the
repository depends on it.
